How it works

Why. Least squares squares the errors, so one wild point 10σ away counts 100 times as much as a typical one and drags the whole curve. Robust methods limit that influence.

M-estimators. Minimise Σ ρ(rᵢ/σ) with a ρ that grows slower than r². This is solved by iteratively reweighted least squares: fit, compute weights wᵢ = ψ(uᵢ)/uᵢ from the scaled residuals, refit with those weights, and repeat. σ is re-estimated each round from the median absolute deviation, σ = MAD/0.6745.

Huber: w = 1 if |u| ≤ 1.345, else 1.345/|u| Bisquare: w = (1 − (u/4.685)²)² if |u| < 4.685, else 0

The tuning constants give 95% efficiency when there are no outliers.

RANSAC. Repeatedly pick d + 1 random points, fit the polynomial through them exactly, and count how many other points lie within the threshold. The largest consensus set is kept and refitted by least squares. It tolerates even a majority of outliers, as long as the inliers agree.

Degree. Chosen by an AICc that uses the robust scale in place of RSS/n, so outliers do not push the degree up.

Flagged points are candidates for checking, not automatically wrong data.
